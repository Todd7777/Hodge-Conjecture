## File 07 — `07-tropical-degeneration.md`

### (1) Overall verdict

The Hodge-theoretic part (§§2–3) is correct but almost entirely standard: Lemma 2.1, Proposition 2.2 and Lemma 2.3 are
the local invariant cycle theorem plus weights and semisimplicity, Lemma 3.1 is the usual Hilbert-scheme countability
argument, and Theorem A, Proposition 3.3 and Theorem D are reformulations that follow in a few lines from these
(Theorem A's "(Comp)" is generic algebraicity spread out over a base change, not a special-fibre criterion). The
tropical part (§4) is correct and is the most substantive: Lemma 4.1 (checked numerically), Theorem B, Corollary 4.3,
Proposition 4.4 and Lemma 4.5 are right; Corollary 4.6 is honestly conditional on (H-trop), and its $n=3$ conclusion is
Brosnan's observation (arXiv:2609.14169, §1). §5 contains a pervasive normalization error: with $\delta(h)=\det h$,
every Weil sixfold has $\delta<0$, so "discriminant $+1$" is empty, and Propositions 5.1(2), 5.2 and everything built on
them speak about nothing. The mathematics survives the substitution "$\delta=+1$" $\to$ "$\delta\neq-1$" (the proof of
5.1(2) shows index 2 exactly when $\delta\not\equiv-1$; the proof of 5.2 never uses $\delta$). One bibliographic entry is
wrong ([KLS]). No claim that HC is proved; target (c) is marked not achieved. **Recommendation: minor revision, with the
discriminant correction mandatory.**

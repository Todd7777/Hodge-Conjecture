## 1. `docs/attacks/01-semiregularity.md`

### 1.1 Overall verdict

The draft correctly reports that the variational Hodge conjecture (VHC) is not proved, and its proofs of the standard parts are correct. But it misstates its central input and overstates what is new. (a) Bloch–Esnault–Kerz's main theorem (arXiv:1310.1773, Thm 1.2) **assumes the Chow–Künneth property (CK)^i of the generic fibre X_η for all even i**; without it the equivalence is proved only for k algebraic over Q (Rem 1.3), and the lift it produces is a compatible element of (lim_n K_0(X_n)) ⊗ Q. The draft states the equivalence with no hypothesis and applies it with k = C. So §3 Step 2, the equality Λ^form = Λ^Hdg in Theorem D(1), and Theorem D(3) ("the only obstruction is algebraization of formal lifts") are unproved for general families, and the §6 sentence saying Theorem D uses no unverified input is false. (b) What is proved is known: Proposition 3.1 and the reduction to curves are BEK's own Appendix A (proof of Prop. A.2: induction on dim S, Artin approximation, transfer). Theorem B follows at once from Buchweitz–Flenner 2003, Thm 5.1, whose proof already lifts the sheaf to every infinitesimal neighbourhood and then uses a versal deformation and Artin approximation, together with a resolution of the component and Corollary A.1. So gap G2 is closed and Theorem B holds unconditionally, but it is not new. Theorem D is a tautology given its definitions. (c) Theorem C (Fourier–Mukai invariance of semiregularity) is conditional on the unverified Hypothesis (H), and it was anticipated by Markman (arXiv:2502.03415v2, Rem 8.3.6), who proves the invariance under the hypotheses of his Lemma 8.3.4 and states the general case as an expectation. Corollary C.2(b) is trivial and needs no (H). (d) Two descriptive statements are wrong: §4's "BF … need all ch_k to stay Hodge" (BF's Thm 5.1 works with I-semiregularity and only the components in I Hodge), and the Kähler test's claim about Voisin's tori (in the same family the Weil classes are algebraic at the algebraic members, by Markman). Recommendation: **major revision**. Restate (BEK) with its hypothesis, relabel Theorem D, attribute Proposition 3.1 and Theorem B, and correct §4(1) and §7.

## 3. `docs/attacks/05-kuga-satake.md`

### 3.1 Overall verdict

Mathematically sound and candid: the top target (KS-HC for all K3 surfaces) is not proved. By standard arguments it is reduced to VHC for the Kuga–Satake family (Theorem A), to one very general surface of each squarefree degree (Cor A.1), and to HC or the Lefschetz standard conjecture B on one compactified total space (Cor A.2). I checked these proofs and found them correct; they are standard, and Cor A.2(b) is the known implication "B ⇒ variational Hodge" (André). Theorem B (which K3-type spaces embed in W_e = H^3 ⊥ ⟨e⟩) is correct and elementary. Example B.2 (T = U ⊥ ⟨10, −2, −4, −20⟩, anisotropic over Q_5 by Springer) checks out, and Propositions B.3–B.5 are correct and easy. The literature input (F_n) is now verified: Floccari 2025 (Geom. Topol. 30 (2026)), Thm 1.2 holds for every n ≥ 2, and Thm 5.11 is exactly the embedding criterion of Cor B.1 (it also gives HC for all powers). So Cor B.1 is Fl25 Thm 5.11 plus Theorem B, G5 is closed, and the consequences "every ρ ≥ 17" and "ρ = 16 with split C^+" hold. Problems: (1) the summary and the claims table say "C^+(T) split iff KS(T) ∼ B^4". Only "split ⇒ B^4" is proved; the converse is likely false for surfaces with real multiplication, since by Schlickewei 2010, Thm 1, KS(T) is isogenous to the fourth power of an abelian fourfold for every rank-6 T with real multiplication by a quadratic field. (2) The checklist claims that the top target would settle the K3 cases of problem P2; that holds only for multiquadratic E (04's Theorem A). (3) The [BEK14] reference has the wrong journal. (4) §2.4(i) and G2 overstate what Buchweitz–Flenner require (BF Thm 5.1 allows I-semiregularity with only the I-components Hodge). (5) Cor B.1 should be attributed to Fl25 Thm 5.11 directly. Recommendation: **minor revision**.

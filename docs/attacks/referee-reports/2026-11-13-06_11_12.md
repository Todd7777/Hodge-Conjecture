### (1) Overall verdict

This is a careful and largely correct file. I found **no wrong mathematical claim**. Its central structural theorem, Theorem B, checks out type by type: the Hodge classes on simple non-CM abelian sixfolds are divisor products plus Weil classes, with the exceptional cases III, IV₁, IV₂ and IV₃. - Shimura's theorem is quoted from memory.

Recommendation: **accept with minor revisions.**

## File 3: `docs/attacks/12-counterexample.md`

### (1) Overall verdict

Most individual proofs are correct:
- Theorem A (specialization through the components);
- the Witt-index and monodromy statements (Theorem B);
- RM K3 families have finite local monodromy (Theorem C);
- Lemma A.3, Prop. F1, Prop. H2;
- the algebra of Theorem E4(a)–(c).

The file has two substantive errors in exactly the places it advertises as its main output.

**(i) The headline candidate family is mislabelled.** The file adopts disc := det H and proves signature (n,n), so det H < 0 for every Weil sixfold. "Sixfolds of discriminant +1" (K1, §3, Cor. B.1, the §0 bullet for B.2, and **Statement S**) is therefore the **empty set**, and Statement S as written is vacuous. File 06 Remark 1.3 already gets this right. The intended family is non-split sixfolds, disc ∈ Q_{<0}/N(K^×) with disc ≠ −1.

**(ii) The "sign condition" is not a test that can fail.** It is offered (E4(c), §0, Remarks E5.1–E5.2) as an HC-implied condition whose violation "would disprove HC". In fact W₀ consists of primitive *Lefschetz* classes. For these, Milne (Ann. Math. 155 (2002), Remark 3.7) proved the Hodge standard conjecture unconditionally. So (−1)^n∫w₀² > 0 always, and by E5.1 N(λ_v) is always a norm.

What survives of the "only non-void mechanism" is the class of λ_v in T¹(Q_ℓ)/T¹(Q). That is precisely the Weil-class, supersingular case of Milne's (weak) rationality conjecture: Milne's §4, Remarks 4.6–4.7 already describe the two Q-structures. G1's "found none in the literature" is therefore wrong.

Novelty is overstated elsewhere too:
- Theorem B's "exact Witt index" is Brosnan's Lemma 9.
- D4 is a one-line application of Principle B.
- The heading "specialization is rationally void" overstates a reduction.

Cor. A.2 needs smooth special-fibre components.

Recommendation: **major revision.** The fixes are textual, but they change the file's stated conclusion and its open Statement S.

# Fermat surfaces S_m: lines versus the full Picard number

`rank(lines)` is the rank of the Gram matrix of the 3m^2 lines (hodge.fermat_lines); `Shioda` is 3(m-1)(m-2)+1+[m even]; `rho` = |B^2_m| + 1 is the Picard number computed from Hodge characters (every Hodge class on a surface is algebraic by Lefschetz (1,1)); `rho - rank` is the dimension of NS (x) Q not spanned by lines (Aoki-Shioda curves are needed there).
